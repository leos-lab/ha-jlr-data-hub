# Advanced

!!! abstract "Optional: most people never need this"
    The integration works on its own. Only read on if you want **one car's data in several places**, or history and
    alerts beyond what Home Assistant keeps.

- [**jlr-server (proxy)**](jlr-server.md): a small server that holds your one JLR credential and shares the car's data
  with several clients. It also adds battery used per trip, history and alerts.
- [**jlr-server API**](api.md): the endpoints, for your own apps and scripts.
